# E3 Portrait Plan

## A - Geometry
- I want to add 120 small dots around the character's head.
- The dots will form a glowing circle, making the character look magical and energetic.

## B - Assets
- I want to draw two simple SVG images myself.
- One will be a yellow star on the left side of the head, and the other will be a blue moon on the right.
- These decorations will make the character look cute and playful.

## C - Color and Curves
- I want to use a gradient that changes from light pink to purple on the character's face.
- I will also use a Bézier curve to create a gentle smile.
- This will make the character look friendly and relaxed.

## Choosing Criteria
I will choose the design that looks the clearest and most interesting at a small size.
